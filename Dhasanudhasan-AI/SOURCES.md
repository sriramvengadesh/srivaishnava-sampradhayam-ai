
# Source Policy

## Priority 1: Koyil.org

Website: https://koyil.org/

Use as the first source to investigate relevant Sri Vaishnava topics.
Find the specific article, text or resource that supports each claim.

## Priority 2: Acharya.org

Website: https://acharya.org/

Use for relevant Acharya-related material and other resources available
on the site. Record exact page or book references.

## Priority 3: Sadagopan.org

Website: https://www.sadagopan.org/

Use for relevant literature, e-books and traditional works.
Record the title, author when available, and exact page or section.

## Source recording rules

For each knowledge entry:

1. Record the exact page or book used.
2. Add its direct URL whenever available.
3. Distinguish a source's actual statements from your own summary.
4. Keep unverified entries marked `needs_review`.
5. Do not fabricate quotations, page numbers or citations.
6. If the primary sources do not cover a topic adequately, use another
   reliable source and clearly identify it as supplementary.
7. Prefer concise summaries and links instead of copying entire works.

The homepage of a website is a directory link, not proof of a particular
claim. Each substantive entry should eventually have its own references.
